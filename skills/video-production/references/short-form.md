# Short-form script structure — hook, value, CTA

> Written after four demo scripts were rejected in a row. Every one of them was
> missing all three of the things below, and this skill had no rule for any of
> them. The shape here is standard short-form video craft; the evidence that it
> matters is our own.

`narrative.md` covers the claim, the language and the six-beat spine — what makes
a **brand film** work. This file covers what makes a **short social video** work,
which is a different job with a different shape.

---

## 1. The shape

```
HOOK        0–3s        why anyone keeps watching
BODY/VALUE  ~70%        what they actually get
CTA         last 3–5s   exactly what to do now
```

**All three are mandatory.** Without the hook it is not watched; without the
value it is not finished; without the CTA it converts nothing and the other two
were wasted.

### The 3-second rule

Open on a **visual event** — a cut, a frame change, a move — under text that
lands hard. Not a fade-in on a title card.

### Stack the hooks

A short video carries three hooks at once, and using more than one multiplies the
chance it works:

| | |
|---|---|
| **verbal** | what they hear in the first 3 seconds |
| **visual** | what they see in the first 3 seconds |
| **written** | what they read in the first 3 seconds |

A silent video still has two of the three and must use both.

---

## 2. Hook categories

Pick one deliberately. Writing an opening line and calling it a hook is how all
four rejected scripts began.

| Category | Use when | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| **comparison** | two options, two eras, two prices | "X used to take a week" |
| **tutorial** | you are about to teach a sequence | "How to [result] in [short time]" |
| **transformation** | a real before/after | "From [old] to [new] in [time]" |
| **educational** | one surprising fact | "Most people don't know [fact]" |
| **mistake** | correcting a common error | "Stop doing [X] — do this instead" |
| **myth** | correcting a belief | "[Belief] is not true, and here is why" |
| **ranking** | several things ordered | "Ranking [category], worst to best" |
| **cost** | a price revealed | "Here is exactly what [X] costs" |
| **challenge** | a goal you set and documented | "I tried [X] for [time]" |
| **story** | a pivotal moment | "I'll never forget the day [moment]" |

Four levers sit under all of them: **curiosity gap · identity · timing ·
provocation.** "If you are a [role] trying to [goal], this is for you" is the
identity lever, and it qualifies the audience in one line.

---

## 3. The body must have a value form

Value has a **form**, not a mood. Pick one and declare it:

| Form | Looks like |
|---|---|
| `steps` | Step 1 · Step 2 · Step 3 |
| `framework` | a named model the viewer can carry away |
| `comparison` | two options side by side |
| `tips` | four short, direct lines |
| `numbers` | one real figure that means something |
| `story_detail` | a real timeline, real amounts, honest feeling |

> If the middle 70% contains no step, no framework, no comparison and no number,
> there is no body — only a longer hook. That was the diagnosis on all four
> rejected scripts.

---

## 4. The CTA must be literal

| Kind | Goal | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| `follow` | audience | "Follow for more [topic]" |
| `engagement` | reach, saves | "Save this for the next time you [X]" |
| `lead` | pipeline | "Comment [word] and I'll send it" · "Link in bio" |
| `sales` | revenue | "Book at [place], [N] slots left" |

Name the button, the word, the link. A CTA the viewer has to interpret is not a
CTA.

---

## 5. Two content modes

| | `teach` | `story` |
|---|---|---|
| Content | tutorials · tips · frameworks · myths · mistakes · comparisons | the origin, a win, a loss, a lesson |
| Builds | authority and demand | connection |
| CTA | `follow` · `lead` · `sales` | `follow` · `engagement` |
| Formats | whiteboard · screen · split-screen · reaction | voiceover · angle changes |

**A product demo is `teach`.** Treating it as a mood piece is a category error —
the story shape with none of the story material. Two of the four rejected scripts
made exactly that mistake.

---

## 6. Ready-made structures

Standard copywriting frames, any of which fits an 18-second cut:

| Model | Beats |
|---|---|
| **AIDA** | problem? → a worse fact → imagine [result] → do this now |
| **PAS** | stop doing X → here is what it costs → the fix |
| **BAB** | before → after → the bridge |
| **Proof · Promise · Plan** | I did [result] for [N] → here is the short version → steps 1-2-3 |
| **Myth · Truth · Reason** | stop believing X → the truth → why |
| **Three things** | 3 things nobody tells you about X → one → two → three |

---

## 7. Line-level rules

- **15 words per line, maximum.**
- **Imperative verbs** — "start", "open", "try". Not the passive voice.
- **Cut every pause.** More cuts keeps the eye awake and watch time up.
- The line has to make the viewer **look smart for sharing it**.

---

## 8. What this file changed in the gate

`scripts/script_check.mjs` fails a script with no declared hook, a hook landing
after 3 seconds, fewer than two stacked hook channels, a body with no value form,
value under 55% of the running time, a missing CTA, a CTA kind that contradicts
the content mode, or any line over 15 words.

Run against the four scripts written before it existed: **not one had a hook, a
value form, or a CTA.**
