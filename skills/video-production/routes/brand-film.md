# Route — brand film / manifesto

**Delegates to:** `/hyperframes` → `/general-video`, with `/hyperframes-creative` for the design
spec and beat planning, and `/media-use` for music.

**This layer adds:** the single-claim discipline, the brand contract, and verification.

## The one test

If the outline carries more than one claim, it is a brochure. Cut until one remains.

A brand film earns its length with **a tension the viewer recognises**, not with more features. The
structure that works: a value the audience holds → the tension that threatens it → the promise.
Features belong in a different video.

## Where these go wrong

- **Efficiency claims instead of elimination claims.** "Saves you two hours" is weaker than "the
  question never reaches you." Choosing between them is a positioning decision — `/hyperframes`
  cannot make it and neither can this skill. Ask.
- **A defensive feature answering an objection nobody raised.** If the script argues with a critic,
  cut it.
- **Music chosen last.** In this genre the track is structural. Resolve it during planning, through
  `/media-use` so the licence lands in the ledger, and plan the beats against it.
- **Original music and recognisable performers are a rights problem**, not a production problem.
  Flag it at brief time or it becomes a legal one at delivery.

## Length

Specialised narrative workflows are strongest at 30–90s and support up to about 3 minutes. Anything
longer routes to `/general-video` directly and plans its own spine.

## Regional note

In some markets a seasonal brand film is its own genre with its own grammar — an original song, an
ensemble cast, a social insight, and a 2–3.5 minute long-form cut that cutdowns are pulled from.
That genre needs composition, casting and rights work that this layer plans (`routes/camera-project.md`)
but does not produce.
